nan - demo commandline Babbit data enrichment [Version 1 (First Contact)]

Usage: nan <command> [-f <filename>] [-cu=<CU field name>]
       [-x=<XC field name>] [-y=<YC field name>]
       [-z=<ZC field name>] [-dom=<Domain field name>]

nan is a tool for enriching geological data (drill hole data and block model). The tool runs a machine learned predictive algorithm on the data and adds additional variables.

Required variables:
- CU = Estimate of copper in percent [0, 100].
- XC = X coordinate of sample.
- YC = Y coordinate of sample.
- ZC = Z coordinate of sample.
- Domain = Interpreted mineralisation domain [0, 1].

The added variables include:
- x_umap = X coordinate of UMAP dimension reduction transformation.
- y_umap = Y coordinate of UMAP dimension reduction transformation.
- FE = Estimate of iron in percent.
- S = Estimate of sulfur in percent.
- AS = Estimate of arsenic in ppm.
- BA = Estimate of barium in percent.
- AL = Estimate of alumina in percent.
- SG = Rock density in g/cm^3.
- R_ = Latent variable representing rock unit.
- G_ = Latent variable representing copper mineralisation grouping.
- CU_Rec = Estimate of copper recovery in percent.
- grp = Latent variable representing rock/material type.

Example:

    nan test
    nan help
    nan pred -file test.csv

For a listing of options, use nan help.
